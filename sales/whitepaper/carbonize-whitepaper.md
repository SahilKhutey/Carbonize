# AI-Designed Solvents for Industrial CO₂ Capture: A Comparative Study with MEA

**Authors**: Carbonize Research Team  
**Version**: 1.0 (December 2024)  
**Status**: Public release  

---

## Abstract
Industrial carbon capture is essential to meeting net-zero targets, but its cost remains a barrier to deployment at gigaton scale. Current amine-based solvents (monoethanolamine, MEA) account for 30-50% of operating costs and degrade by 15% annually.

This paper presents Carbonize's AI-designed solvent platform, combining quantum chemistry, machine learning, and Bayesian optimization to discover new absorption solvents in 6 months — 10x faster than traditional R&D. We validate our approach against 30+ published datasets, achieving 92.4% prediction accuracy, and demonstrate SOLV-0237 outperforming MEA: 32% lower energy, 18% higher capacity, 3x slower degradation, and 90% higher absorption rate.
